Meet Tiffany

Deep roots. Clear-eyed problem solving.

Tiffany Boccieri is a former eDiscovery attorney with deep, longstanding ties to the South Orange County community she's asking to represent.

Her legal background trained her to dig into the facts, weigh evidence carefully, and reach conclusions that hold up — the same approach she wants to bring to the decisions Laguna Hills City Council makes on behalf of residents.

Tiffany believes local government works best when parents and everyday families have a real seat at the table, not just a public comment slot. She's running for District 4 to make sure the concerns raised at kitchen tables and school pickup lines actually reach City Hall.

Priorities

What Tiffany is fighting for in District 4

A few of the issues driving Tiffany's campaign — grounded in what she's hearing directly from District 4 neighbors.

01

Safer Neighborhood Streets

Residents on Alameda Avenue in Stratford Ridge have raised real concerns about traffic enforcement in their neighborhood. Tiffany is pushing for the city to follow through with consistent enforcement and traffic-calming measures — not just a study.

02

A Voice for Parents & Families

Tiffany wants city decisions that affect families — from parks to schools-adjacent traffic safety — made with parents in the room from the start, not informed after the fact.

03

A Government That Shows Its Work

Tiffany's legal background means she reads the fine print. She'll push for clear, honest communication from the city and budget and policy decisions residents can actually follow.
