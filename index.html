Meet Tiffany

An Attorney's Focus. A Neighbor's Heart.

Tiffany Boccieri grew up in South Orange County and chose to raise her own family here - drawn to the safe neighborhoods, parks, and close-knit community that make Laguna Hills unique.

She has never been the kind of person who wants to merely exist in a community - she wants to help build one. Tiffany believes in equity, not just equality: not every child, and not every family, starts from the same place or has the same tools to work with, and a rising tide only floats all boats if everyone actually has a boat to begin with.

As a California-licensed attorney, Tiffany knows that sound decisions require careful analysis, active listening, and accountability - whether reviewing a city budget, evaluating a development proposal, or analyzing a traffic study. That same instinct for service shows up closer to home, too: she currently serves as a Girl Scout troop leader, a room parent, and a PTA board member.

Tiffany believes every resident deserves a strong voice in the decisions that shape Laguna Hills' future, and she's running for District 4 to bring service, integrity, and accountability to the City Council.

Priorities

What Tiffany Will Focus On in District 4

Tiffany's priorities for City Council are focused on responsible growth, safe and reliable infrastructure, public safety, resident privacy, accountable District 4 representation, and careful stewardship of public funds.

Responsible Growth & Housing

Tiffany supports meeting Laguna Hills' housing obligations while protecting the qualities that make our neighborhoods a great place to live. She will carefully evaluate major development proposals for traffic, parking, infrastructure capacity, fiscal impact, neighborhood compatibility, and long-term benefit to the community - while supporting housing options that allow people at different stages of life to remain part of Laguna Hills.

Infrastructure, Traffic & Resilience

Tiffany will prioritize the infrastructure residents rely on every day - roads, traffic signals, sidewalks, bike connections, parks, and public facilities. She supports data-driven improvements that make neighborhood streets and school routes safer, reduce unnecessary congestion, maintain our public spaces, and ensure that infrastructure investments are cost-effective, durable, and prepared for the future.

Safe Streets & Strong Response

Laguna Hills' safety depends on strong police, fire, emergency response, and crime prevention. Tiffany supports careful oversight of the City's partnerships with the Orange County Sheriff's Department and Orange County Fire Authority, along with effective coordination with county behavioral-health, outreach, school-safety, and other community resources when specialized services can help prevent a problem from becoming a public-safety crisis.

District 4 Representation

Laguna Hills' new by-district election system gives District 4 its own representative on the City Council. Tiffany lives in Stratford Ridge in District 4 and is committed to being accessible and accountable to the people she represents - bringing neighborhood concerns to City Hall while considering the citywide impact of every vote. That includes issues affecting neighborhoods around Paseo de Valencia, La Paz, Moulton and Alicia, school access and traffic around Valencia Elementary, and the businesses and public spaces that serve District 4. View the official District 4 map.

Fiscal Stewardship & Transparency

Tiffany's legal background means she reads the fine print - and knows how to recognize when special interests are working an angle instead of the public's. She will scrutinize city budgets, contracts, development agreements, and major spending proposals, asking about long-term costs, financial risks, and whether taxpayers are receiving clear value. Tiffany's campaign is primarily self-funded, and she's declining contributions and endorsements from special interest groups - so her only obligation on the council is to District 4 constituents, not donors. She also believes residents should be able to understand major City Council decisions, and will work to explain important votes and financial issues in straightforward, plain language.

Protecting Resident Privacy

The Flock license-plate cameras installed around Laguna Hills photograph every vehicle that drives past, whether or not it is connected to a crime. Tiffany opposes renewing the city's Flock contract and will vote against it, and she will require strict public oversight - including a warrant before anyone, the city included, can search resident data - for any surveillance technology the city considers.
