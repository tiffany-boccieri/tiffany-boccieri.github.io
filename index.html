Meet Tiffany

Deep roots. Clear-eyed problem solving.

Tiffany Boccieri grew up in South Orange County and chose to raise her own family here - drawn to the safe neighborhoods, parks, and close-knit community that make Laguna Hills unique.

As a California-licensed attorney, mother, and community volunteer leader, she knows that sound decisions require careful analysis, active listening, and accountability - whether reviewing a city budget, evaluating a development proposal, or analyzing a traffic study.

Tiffany believes every resident deserves a strong voice in the decisions that shape Laguna Hills' future, and she's running for District 4 to bring service, integrity, and accountability to the City Council.

Priorities

What Tiffany is fighting for in District 4

Our city faces real challenges that require thoughtful strategy and a commitment to public oversight. Here's where Tiffany is ready to lead.

Smart Growth & Housing Affordability

Tiffany is committed to meeting our housing goals by prioritizing affordable options so young families, local workers, and seniors can afford to stay in Laguna Hills - and to smart, transit-oriented development that minimizes traffic and preserves our open spaces.

Climate Resilience & Sustainable Infrastructure

Modernizing our city means preparing for the future: upgrading aging infrastructure, updating traffic management networks, investing in green energy, expanding safe walking and biking paths, and protecting our local parks.

Comprehensive Public Safety

Keeping Laguna Hills one of Southern California's safest communities requires a holistic approach - full support for our local first responders, plus expanded community-based programs, including mental health crisis response teams and youth outreach.

Uniting a Changing Voting System

As Laguna Hills transitions to district-based voting, new geographic boundaries shouldn't divide us. Tiffany will be a voice for all of Laguna Hills, keeping the entire city connected and collaborative.
